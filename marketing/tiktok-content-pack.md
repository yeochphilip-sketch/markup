# MARKUP TikTok / Instagram Reels Content Pack

**Product:** MARKUP — AI O-Level Humanities (History + Social Studies) practice simulator with LORMS grading.
**Audience:** Singapore Sec 3–4 students (15–16), studying for GCE O-Levels. Secondary audience: parents (different content).
**Window:** 60-day sprint to O-Levels (prelims → exams). Post at least 1/day, ideally 1–2.
**Voice:** Like a smart senior who aced the exam — not a corporation. Short sentences. SG-English is fine. No corporate polish.
